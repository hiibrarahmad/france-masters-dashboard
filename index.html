The fee rules (verified 9 Oct 2026 on official pages)

  • Public university master, non-EU: €3,950/yr in 2026-27 (EU students pay €255). Licence €2,902. service-public.gouv.fr
  • Exemptions are capped by Decree 2026-385 (19 May 2026): max 30% of non-EU students in 2026-27, 25% in 2027-28 (your intake), 20% after. Treat €255 as a bonus, budget €3,950.
  • Public engineering schools (Diplôme d'ingénieur): EU rate €630. Most schools charge non-EU students €3,950 for the engineering cycle (e.g. INSA Toulouse, ENIM); a few (e.g. Tours) do not.
  • 2027-28 amounts are not published yet – fees usually rise slightly with inflation. All figures here are 2026-27.
  • Extra every year: CVEC ≈ €105, living costs €700–1,100/month (visa proof of funds ≈ €615/month).

  • Université de Tours: 2027-28 policy published – 25% cap, on request.

How to apply for September 2027

Written for: a 4-year bachelor's (Bac+4) with a few years of embedded / biomedical-device experience.

  1. Now – shortlist 10–15 programs. Start with “Best for me”, star the ones you like. Mix 5–7 safe M1 options with a few M2 / reach programs.
  2. Language test. English-taught: IELTS 6.5+ (or TOEFL). French-taught: DELF/TCF B2 – French-taught public masters are the cheapest and most numerous.
  3. Documents. Degree + transcripts (attested), CV, experience letters, statement of purpose per program linking your own embedded / biomedical work to its courses, 2 recommendation letters, portfolio (firmware, signal processing).
  4. Apply on Études en France (through your country's Campus France office) – open from 1 Oct 2026 to 15 Mar 2027. Many programs close earlier in practice – aim to submit by January.
  5. Where it says “separate application” – also apply on the school's own site (302 programs need this). Polytech-network programs go through their own portal too.
  6. Ask for a fee exemption at the same time as admission, and ask each university whether you'll be enrolled as formation initiale (not continuing education, which costs more after a 2+ year study break).
  7. Campus France interview → accept an offer → visa (proof of funds ≈ €615/month, accommodation, insurance).

If your grades are average

French public masters select mostly on grades, so apply widely. Strengthen the file with a sharp statement of purpose, strong experience letters and a good language score. Programs that only open the final year (M2) are a stretch – keep M1 programs as your main options.
